# ST-S200 mini ionizing air blower — source notes

Catalogue page 14. Provenance and rules: [`README.md`](README.md).
Product page: `/products/static-eliminator/st-s200`.

## What the catalogue page says

A miniature ionizing blower for a single workstation or for building into
equipment. **Pulsating DC corona.** Axial fan with **three speeds — low,
medium, high**. **Tungsten-alloy emitters.** Front and rear covers come off for
cleaning. Supplied with a dedicated external adapter.

## Two contradictions in the source — recorded, not resolved

These are in the PDF itself. Neither is to be "corrected" into whichever
reading looks more sensible.

**1. The Ion Balance field is not a voltage.** The parameter table's ion-balance
cell reads **300 mm × 3 s** — a distance and a time. Ion balance is measured in
volts, so the field does not give one.

  - Do **not** substitute ±3 V, ±30 V or any other invented offset.
  - Do **not** present the cell as a decay-time specification either. An earlier
    pass on this site did exactly that, giving the page a "Decay time at 300 mm
    — within 3 s" row. That row has been removed: reading a mislabelled cell as
    a decay figure is an interpretation, not a published value.
  - The page now says the offset voltage is not published for this model and
    that the ion-balance field carries a distance and a time instead.

**2. The waveform figure and the table disagree on voltage.** The positive and
negative high-voltage diagram on the page is annotated **±3 kV / ±7 kV**, while
the parameter table gives **±5000 V**. This is a conflict inside the catalogue
itself — not merely the site's own JPEG being wrong, which is how it was first
recorded here.

  - The public page states only the table's ±5000 V.
  - The figure's values are not published, not generalised, and not used to
    support any other model. The same applies to `operating-modes.jpg` in this
    repository, which names no model and reads ±7 kV.

## Footnotes in the figure are citations, not test evidence

The charge-decay figure carries **2 m/s**, **ANSI/ESD STM3.1**,
**SJ/T 11446-2013** and **Trek 150 kV electrostatic tester** as printed text.

These are the source's own annotations. They are **not** a certification, a
compliance statement or a test report, and the site does not present them as
one. Whether the product was tested to those standards, by whom, and with what
result, is unknown.

## Not published — to confirm with the factory

1. **The ion balance (offset voltage)**, properly — the number that field was
   meant to hold.
2. **Which voltage is correct**, ±5000 V or the figure's ±3 kV / ±7 kV, and what
   each refers to.
3. **Conditions behind ≤1.5 s** — initial and cut-off voltage, fan speed, plate
   size, ambient temperature and humidity.
4. **Decay, airflow and noise per fan speed.** Only single values are given, and
   a buyer choosing a speed needs all three across the three settings.
5. **What the 450 × 450 mm coverage means** — at what distance, and to what
   residual voltage.
6. **Positive and negative decay separately.** The ST101A, ST104A and ST1200
   publish them apart; the ST-S200 gives one combined figure.
7. **Ozone sampling conditions**, and whether 0.05 ppm is steady-state.
8. **The adapter's own rating and part number**, and whether it is orderable
   separately.

## Page review — what was changed and what was left alone

**Changed:**

- The inferred "Decay time at 300 mm" row is gone, and the ion-balance row now
  says what the source actually contains.
- **Coverage and decay are no longer one claim.** The hero, the body, the meta
  description, the shared catalogue entry that feeds the menu blurb and the
  card chips, and the `llms.txt` line all said the unit "clears a 450 × 450 mm
  area in under 1.5 s". The catalogue gives ≤1.5 s **at one point, 150 mm in
  front of the outlet**, and publishes the 450 × 450 mm area with **no distance
  and no acceptance condition**. Two figures, now stated as two, with the
  single-point condition on the decay and no time attached to the area.

**Left alone deliberately.** The page already splits fan and ion-generator
power rather than totalling them, already writes dB rather than dB(A), already
attaches the 150 mm distance to the ozone figure, already lists the three fan
speeds, the removable covers and the adapter's input and output, and already
states ±5000 V from the table rather than the figure. The FAQ answer on
coverage already says it is an area rather than a working distance and attaches
no time to it. The safety wording about the decoupled discharge barrel is the
catalogue's own construction description and stays as it is.
